    Pricing

    Explore pricing options for Pods, including on-demand pricing and storage costs. Review setup, configuration, and operations guidance for Runpod Pods.

    Runpod offers custom pricing plans for large scale and enterprise workloads. Contact our sales team to learn more.
    Pods are billed by the second for compute and storage, with no fees for data ingress or egress. Find the latest GPU pricing on the Runpod console during Pod deployment.

    ​
    Pricing options

    ​
    On-demand

    Pay-as-you-go pricing. Resources are dedicated to your Pod and cannot be displaced by other users.
    You must have at least one hour’s worth of credits for your selected configuration to deploy an on-demand instance.

    ​
    Savings plans

    Savings plans are deprecated and can no longer be purchased. If you bought a savings plan before deprecation, it stays active until its expiration date. The plan applies to your Pods of the same GPU type and covers GPU compute costs only. Storage costs are billed at standard rates. Plans are non-refundable.

    ​
    Storage pricing

    Storage typeRunning PodStopped PodNotes
    Container disk$0.10/GB/monthNot chargedTemporary; erased when Pod stops
    Volume disk$0.10/GB/month$0.20/GB/monthPersistent; retained until Pod deleted
    Network volume$0.07/GB/month (< 1TB)$0.07/GB/monthPermanent; portable between Pods
    $0.05/GB/month (> 1TB)$0.05/GB/month
    Storage is billed per-second for container and volume disks, and hourly for network volumes. You are not charged if the host is unavailable.
    Runpod is not designed for long-term cloud storage. Back up critical data to your local machine or a dedicated storage provider.

    ​
    Account limits

    • Minimum balance: Pods are automatically stopped when your account balance reaches $0. Pods with a network volume are stopped and their data is preserved, while Pods without a network volume are terminated and their data cannot be recovered. To avoid interruptions, enable low balance notifications or set up auto-pay.
    • Spend limit: Default limit of $80/hour across all resources. Contact support to increase.

    ​
    Track your costs

    • Existing savings plans: If you have an active savings plan, monitor its commitment period and expiration date in the Savings plans section.
    • Billing: Track usage and charges in the Billing section.
